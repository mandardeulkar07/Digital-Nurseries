About the community project

Helping plant lovers and local nurseries find each other.

Digital Nursery is a community engagement project designed to share easy-to-understand information about common household plants and make local nursery details easier to find. Instead of selling or shipping plants online, the platform helps visitors learn about plants and connect with vendors directly.
